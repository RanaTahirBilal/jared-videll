A Career in Two Uniforms

About Dr. Videll

Jared S. Videll earned a Bachelor of Education from the University of Miami and a Doctor of Osteopathy from the Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine in 1976. He served his internal medicine residency at Atlantic City Medical Center, rising to chief resident, then held a dual fellowship in cardiovascular disease at Albert Einstein Medical Center and Deborah Heart and Lung Center.

In 2010, after more than three decades in practice, he joined the United States Army Reserve at the age of 55. He retired at the rank of Lieutenant Colonel, having served as Deputy Commander of the 48th Combat Support Hospital in Kuwait.

Of a career full of highlights, Marquis Who’s Who records that he is most proud of his service in uniform.

Nineteen Seventy-Six Onward

Career Timeline

Following a Bachelor of Education from the University of Miami, Dr. Videll completed his osteopathic medical degree in Philadelphia.

Internal medicine internship and residency, concluding as chief resident.

A dual fellowship in cardiovascular disease, followed by a research fellowship in nuclear cardiology at Deborah Heart and Lung Center in Browns Mills, New Jersey. He remained involved there through 1984 as director of employee health services.

Cardiology within a programme built around diet and exercise intervention.

Director of the clinical laboratory, and concurrently director and co-chairperson of intensive care and the cardiac stress laboratory at the nearby Towanda Memorial Hospital.

Responsible for house staff across the intensive care and cardiac care units throughout the 1990s.

He held both roles until 2010, closing more than three decades of continuous clinical practice.

He entered the Army Reserve at the age of 55. He would serve as Deputy Commander of the 48th Combat Support Hospital in Kuwait, supervising outlying clinics and clinical services, and retire at the rank of Lieutenant Colonel.
